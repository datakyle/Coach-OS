# Drill list

Every drill we run, pulled from 18 past practice plans (last season and this
season). Claude only uses drills from this list unless Coach asks for something
new. Team level only: no player names.

Common pieces: **Victory Lap** = winners run a full-sprint down and back, 1
point on the day's tally; losers clap them in. **Team 1 / Team 2** = the day's
teams. **Split stations** = Returners at the main basket, New guys at the far
basket, horn at the end.

## Every practice

| Drill | Mins | How we run it |
|---|---|---|
| Circle Up | 5 | Champion asks his question. Name the focus and two non-negotiables. Winners run is on. Split Team 1 / Team 2 and station groups. |
| Game Warmup | 10 | Dynamic warmup into partner passing and full-speed layups, one team per basket. Say the receiver's name; silent pass is a redo. Last 3 min: first team to 10 straight makes each side runs the Victory Lap (or a Mikan race). |
| Syracuse | 15 | FL, HC, BL sequences with SH (shuffle) and BP (backpedal). Sets 1 and 2 one day, Sets 3 and 4 the next. Team vs team. Partner rule: not done until your partner is done. Group calls the variation out loud. |
| Pressure Free Throws / Earn Your Lap | 5 to 10 | Everyone shoots 2, team says the shooter's name. Round 1: any miss, whole team runs. Round 2: make both and run the Victory Lap. Count the tally; champion picks next Circle Up question. |

## Defense

| Drill | Mins | How we run it |
|---|---|---|
| Shell drill (pack line) | 12 | 5 on 5, coaches fill in. Stages 1 to 4 from the Pack Line handout. Ball, Gap, Help called out loud on every pass. Force middle. |
| Closeout and Recover | 12 | 6 min 1v1 closeouts from help (2 dribbles, games to 3). 6 min 2v2 help and recover: open kick-out is 3 and the defense runs. |
| Closeout Drives | 7 to 8 | Defender passes and closes out, offense gets 2 dribbles. Games to 3. |
| Wing Closeout and Shuffles | 7 to 15 | Choppy steps, early hand. Shooter: close and hands up. Driver: hands to the side, watch the drive. |
| Vegas Closeouts | 7 to 10 | Sprint the first two-thirds, then short chop steps. Get low, two hands up. Push him middle, into help. |
| 2v2 Screen Defense | 7 | Live ball screen and down screen. Screener's man calls it early. Over, under, switch. Talk first or the rep doesn't count. Games to 5. |
| Stance and Slide into Closeout Shooting | 7 | 30 sec slides, then 5 catch-and-shoots from the elbow, partner closes out on the last two. |
| Gap Theory Review | 7 | Make 2 guard 1, make the mismatch, drive bad closeouts, 2v1 half court. |

## Rebounding

| Drill | Mins | How we run it |
|---|---|---|
| Box Out Fundamentals | 12 | Teach slow: find, hit first, reverse pivot, hold 2 sec, two hands, chin, outlet. Pairs on the whistle, then Circle Box Out (keep him off the ball 3 sec), then coach shoots 1v1. 3 straight wins runs. |
| Rebounding Toughness | 12 | 3v3, coach shoots, offense crashes. Defense needs 3 straight boards to get off; an offensive board resets to zero. |
| 2v2 Rebound and Score | 7 | Coach shoots. Putback is 2, defensive board plus outlet is 1. Games to 5. |
| Tip Drill into Chin and Outlet | 7 | 5 tips, grab with two hands and finish. Then chin it and pivot away from a swiper, outlet to the wing. |

## Offense and shooting

| Drill | Mins | How we run it |
|---|---|---|
| Offense Teach: 5 Out Pass and Cut | 12 | Three rules: pass and cut, fill the open spot, drive if nobody is open. Walk it, then full speed. Yell stop; whoever is standing still says where he should be. |
| Offense Reps: 5v0 to 3v3 | 13 | 10 passes with a cut and fill, no dribble. Then 3v3: layup off a cut is 2, pass with no cut is a turnover. |
| Spacing Teach: 5 Out Drive and Kick | 12 | Stay about 15 feet apart. On a drive, the two closest slide one spot away. 4 on 0, then live. |
| 2v2 Drive and Kick | 7 | Touch the paint before any shot. Kick-out 3 counts 2. First to 7. |
| 2v1 Drive and Kick | 6 | Helper helps: kick to the corner. Stays: finish. First to 7. |
| Pass and Cut Finishing | 6 | Top to wing and cut, bounce pass back, finish. 90-second rounds. |
| Catch, Rip and Go | 7 | Jump stop, rip low, one hard dribble to a layup. Add a closeout: runs at you, drive; sits, shoot. |
| Read the Screen Shooting | 7 | Chair is the screen. Passer calls POP, CURL or FADE. 3s count 2. |
| 5 and 60 | 7 to 12 | Two-pass weave into a 3. Made 3 is 2, putback 1. 5 minutes to reach 60 (40 early on). Hit it and Coach runs. |
| Pair Shootout | 7 | 5 spots (or zone spots), 60 seconds, partner rebounds. Higher pair runs. |
| Kentucky Shooting | 5 | 3 lines of 3, middle up and half court. |
| Knockout | 10 | 2 balls. FT line, elbow, top of the key 3. |
| Hot Shot | Extra | 60 seconds, 5 spots worth 1 to 5. |

## Zone offense

| Drill | Mins | How we run it |
|---|---|---|
| 2-3 Zone Teach | 10 | Board, then walk it: three holes (free throw line, short corners, across the top). Players name all three back. |
| Split Station Shooting (zone spots) | 14 | Free throw line, short corners, wings. |
| Install: High and Skip | 16 | Dummy first, then live with coaches in an honest 2-3. |
| Live vs the 2-3 | 8 to 10 | Coaches in the zone. Called play basket is 2, short corner or skip 3. Shooting before the zone moves is a turnover. |
