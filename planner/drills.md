# Drill list

Every drill we run. Claude only uses drills from this list unless Coach asks for
something new. Prefilled from past practices; Coach fills the blanks.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Circle Up | 5 | Focus, question of the day, split teams | | Yes |

| Teach block | 10 to 12 | New concept | Board first, then walk it on the floor | Yes |

| Live vs the 2-3 | 8 to 10 | Zone offense | Coaches in the zone; shooting before the zone moves is a turnover | Yes |
